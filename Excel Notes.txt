2 - INDEX, MATCH - When VLOOKUP doesnot work
Example:-  
	Data:
	A2:A6 → Employee Names
	B2:B6 → Salary
	
	=INDEX(B2:B6, MATCH("Rahul", A2:A6, 0))
Meaning:
	MATCH finds the row where “Rahul” is
	INDEX returns the salary from that same row

6 - SUMIF
Example :-
Goal: Sum sales where Region = “East”.

Data:
A2:A6 → Region
B2:B6 → Sales
Formula-
=SUMIF(A2:A6, "East", B2:B6)
7 - SUMIFS
Example:-
Goal: Sum sales where

Region = “East”

Product = “Laptop”

Data:
A2:A6 → Region
B2:B6 → Product
C2:C6 → Sales
Formula-
=SUMIFS(C2:C6, A2:A6, "East", B2:B6, "Laptop")
8 - COUNTIF
Example:-
Goal: Count how many employees have “HR” department.

Data:
A2:A10 → Department
Formula-
=COUNTIF(A2:A10, "HR")
9 - COUNTIFS
Goal: Count employees where

Department = “HR”

Status = “Active”

Data:
A2:A10 → Department
B2:B10 → Status
Formula-
=COUNTIFS(A2:A10, "HR", B2:B10, "Active")
