# Data Analytics Preparation Notes

These are the notes I created and collected while preparing for **Data Analytics roles and interviews**. They cover the core topics that I found most relevant during my preparation and through my interview experiences.

I've organized the material into **six key areas**:

1. **SQL** – Queries, joins, CTEs, window functions, aggregations, and other commonly asked concepts.
2. **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX, visualization, and important BI concepts.
3. **Tableau** – Dashboarding, calculations, filters, and frequently discussed concepts.
4. **Excel** – Functions, data analysis, lookups, pivot tables, and practical analytical techniques.
5. **Python for Data Analytics** – Python fundamentals and concepts relevant to data analysis.
6. **Cloud** – Basic cloud concepts and services that are useful for a Data Analyst.

### Who is this for?

These notes are primarily intended for:

* People preparing for **Data Analyst interviews**
* Beginners trying to understand the **core skills required for Data Analytics**
* Anyone looking for a **quick revision resource** before an interview
* Professionals looking to refresh their fundamentals

### A Small Disclaimer

These are **personal preparation notes**, not a complete course or a replacement for hands-on practice. Some topics may be more detailed than others, and the notes are based on what I found useful during my own preparation and interview experiences.

I hope they help you structure your preparation and save some time.

**Good luck with your preparation! 🚀**
